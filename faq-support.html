FAQ & Support

Updated September 25, 2026

What is Pure Bite?

Pure Bite is an intermittent fasting tracker for iPhone. You pick a plan (like 16:8), start and end fasts with a live countdown, see history and streaks, and browse recipes when your eating window opens. It is not a calorie counter or medical app.

Do I need an account?

No. Fasting history, preferences, and saved recipes stay on your device. There is no account registration.

Is the timer free?

Yes. Core plans including 16:8 are free to start. Pure Bite Plus unlocks advanced and custom protocols. Purchases use Apple In-App Purchase (via RevenueCat).

Will Pure Bite send me notifications?

Only if you turn on fasting reminders. The system permission appears after you choose to enable them — not on first launch.

Do recipes work offline?

The fasting timer works offline. Recipe search needs a network connection; saved recipes remain available on your device.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pure Bite does not diagnose or treat conditions. Intermittent fasting is not right for everyone. Talk to a doctor before changing how you eat, especially if you are pregnant, have a history of eating disorders, or manage a medical condition.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Pure Bite → You / Profile → Restore purchases (also available on the Plus paywall).
