Privacy Policy

Updated September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aleksandr Riakhin (“Pure Bite”, “we”) handles information when you use the Pure Bite iOS app and this website.

What Pure Bite is

Pure Bite is an intermittent fasting tracker with optional recipes for your eating window. You do not create an account to use the app.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is not uploaded to Pure Bite servers:

  • Fasting plans, active session, history, and streaks
  • Reminder preferences
  • Saved recipes and shopping list items
  • App settings (units, themes where applicable)

Deleting the app removes this local data.

Information we may process

  • Device & usage analytics. Anonymous product analytics via Firebase Analytics (app opens, screen views, feature events, crash/diagnostic signals). No advertising identifier tracking for ads.
  • Recipe catalog. The app may download Pure Bite’s owned recipe catalog (recipes, locales, ingredients, images) from Pure Bite’s CDN so you can browse and open recipes. After a catalog generation is installed, recipe reads can work without further network access.
  • Purchases. Plus subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. We use RevenueCat to validate entitlements. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase data under their own policies.
  • Website. This site may use Google Analytics (property G-SLMY5TN10Y) for anonymous traffic metrics.

Notifications

If you enable fasting reminders, iOS delivers local notifications. Pure Bite does not send push marketing notifications.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or phone as part of an account (there is no account)
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data
  • A food diary or calorie log

Third parties

  • Google / Firebase Analytics
  • Cloudflare (recipe catalog CDN / Worker)
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple (App Store / In-App Purchase)

Your choices

You can limit analytics through iOS Settings, disable notifications anytime, and manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscriptions. To ask questions about privacy, contact xander1100001@gmail.com.

Children

Pure Bite is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app or site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
