Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

IMPORTANT: This Privacy Policy explains how Alexander Ryakhin ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the PureBite mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Introduction

PureBite is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the App. We believe in transparency and want you to understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We do NOT collect or transmit the following data:

  • Your saved recipes
  • Your shopping list items
  • Your search queries or browsing history
  • Your personal preferences or settings

All of this information is stored exclusively on your device using CoreData and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

2.2 Analytics Information

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the App. This information includes:

  • App Usage Data: Which screens you visit, session duration, and app opens
  • Feature Usage: Which features you use (e.g., search, recipe saving, shopping list)
  • Performance Metrics: App load times, response times, and error rates
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version (anonymized)
  • Error Reports: Technical errors and crashes (without personal information)

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Your saved recipes or shopping list contents
  • Your search queries or browsing history
  • Your device location or GPS coordinates
  • Your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • Payment information (the App is free)
  • Biometric data

3. How We Use Information

We use the anonymous analytics data we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • App Improvement: To understand which features are most popular and identify areas for improvement
  • Bug Fixes: To quickly identify and resolve technical issues and errors
  • Performance Optimization: To improve app speed, responsiveness, and reliability
  • User Experience Enhancement: To make the App more intuitive and user-friendly

We do NOT use your information for:

  • Advertising or marketing purposes
  • Sharing with third parties for their marketing purposes
  • Creating user profiles for advertising
  • Tracking you across other apps or websites

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

4.1 Firebase Analytics (Google)

Firebase Analytics is provided by Google LLC and is used to collect anonymous usage analytics. Firebase Analytics:

  • Collects anonymous usage data as described above
  • Does not collect personally identifiable information
  • Is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Uses industry-standard security measures

You can learn more about how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4.2 Spoonacular API

We use the Spoonacular API to provide recipe data. When you search for recipes or view recipe details:

  • Your search queries are sent to Spoonacular to retrieve recipe data
  • Spoonacular may collect information about API usage in accordance with their privacy policy
  • Spoonacular's Privacy Policy: https://spoonacular.com/privacy
  • We do not control how Spoonacular uses this data

4.3 Apple App Store

If you download the App from the Apple App Store, Apple may collect certain information in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to this information.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share anonymous analytics data with Firebase Analytics (Google) as described above
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction

We do NOT share your locally stored data (recipes, shopping lists) with anyone, as this data never leaves your device.

6. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

6.1 Control Analytics Collection

You can control analytics data collection in several ways:

  • iOS Settings: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and disable "Share iPhone Analytics"
  • App Settings: Analytics collection is enabled by default, but you can disable it through your device's privacy settings
  • No Impact on Functionality: Disabling analytics will not affect the core functionality of the App

6.2 Access and Delete Your Data

Since your recipes and shopping lists are stored locally on your device:

  • You have full control over this data
  • You can delete individual items at any time
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the App
  • We cannot access or delete this data as it never leaves your device

6.3 Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at xander1100001@gmail.com.

6.4 Your Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: Request information about what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at xander1100001@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Local Data: Your recipes and shopping lists are stored securely on your device using iOS's built-in security features
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous analytics data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • No Centralized Storage: We do not maintain servers that store your personal recipe data

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at xander1100001@gmail.com, and we will take steps to delete such information.

If you are between 13 and 18 years old, you must have your parent's or guardian's permission to use the App.

10. International Data Transfers

If you are using the App from outside the United States, please note that:

  • Anonymous analytics data may be transferred to and processed in the United States by Firebase (Google)
  • Recipe API requests may be processed by Spoonacular's servers, which may be located outside your country
  • Your locally stored data (recipes, shopping lists) never leaves your device and is not transferred internationally

By using the App, you consent to the transfer of anonymous analytics data to the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. However, third-party services we use (Firebase Analytics) may use cookies or similar technologies. These are subject to the privacy policies of those third parties.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated Privacy Policy in the App or on our website
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Providing notice through the App or other reasonable means

Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to the changes, you should stop using the App.
