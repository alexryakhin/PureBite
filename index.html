How it works

One job: stay in your window

No calorie diary. No barcode camera. Just a clear timer for intermittent fasting — and help deciding what to eat when the fast ends.

Pick a plan

Start with 16:8, or choose another schedule that fits your day. Custom protocols are available on Plus.

Start or end your fast

See time remaining on a live ring. Optional reminders — only after you choose to enable them.

Build a streak

History and a simple streak keep you coming back without turning fasting into a social feed.

Eating window Know what to cook when the timer flips.

Recipes when it’s time to eat

Pure Bite keeps recipes secondary to fasting — they light up for your eating window: break-fast meals, high protein, quick options, and a shopping list.

  • Break-fast and high-protein ideas
  • Under 30 minutes when you’re hungry now
  • Saved recipes and shopping list on device

Start your next 16:8

Free to begin. Core timer plans like 16:8 stay startable — Plus is optional for advanced schedules.

Pure Bite is not medical advice and is not a medical device. Intermittent fasting is not right for everyone — talk to a doctor before changing how you eat.
